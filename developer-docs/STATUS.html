CI health — date-pinned liturgy canary

Remote CI on main runs green via the self-hosted runner (the historical "GitHub-Actions billing-blocked" banner is stale). However, the DailyReferenceComparisonTest resolves its date as LocalDate.now() — it compares today's assembled liturgy against a committed reference fixture. CI was green through 2026-06-28 and flipped red on 2026-06-29 = the feast of Sts. Peter & Paul, a major fixed feast that the test only exercises once a year.

Reproduced locally, the only mismatch is [FEAST_CLASS]: the Ustav row 29.VI declares feast_class: "Господнє свято" (rank 6) but V2 computes POLYELEOS (rank 3), and the promotion-only comparator flags the downgrade. Readings (Ап 2 Кор 193, Єв Мт 67), cycle (Octoechos wk 6), and tone (4) all match the reference. Peter & Paul is canonically a great apostles' feast, not a Lord's feast, so the Ustav tag may itself be the imperfect side; there is also no feast_fixed_* service definition for it. User-facing impact is minor (correct readings/tone/cycle; only the feast-rank tier differs on one day a year). Tracked in #1540 — both the classification question and the structural LocalDate.now() test-fragility (a "today"-based canary makes main's CI calendar-dependent).
