Purpose

Despite the module name, :feature:rosary-v2 owns the entire Молитви prayers hub — the second tab on the five-tab bottom rail (Дім · Літургія · Молитви · Спільнота · Більше). Where :feature:liturgy-v2 covers the Divine Liturgy itself, the Prayers hub covers devotional practice around it: the rosary, the Great Akathist, the seven-weekday Canon of the Day, and a searchable library of daily and personal prayers.

The landing surface is a categorized library (PrayersListRoute, titled «Молитви» under the БІБЛІОТЕКА eyebrow) grouping rows into four sections: Щодня (Щоденні / Ранкові / Вечірні molitvy), Свята і пости, Псалтир і канони (today's kathisma, a «Усі 20 кафизм» link, Канон дня, Акафіст Богородиці), and Особисті (Вервиця + intention placeholders). Several rows are intentionally inert — they surface the information architecture but their onClick is null until the matching content ships in molutovnyk_parsed.json (e.g. «Божественна Літургія», «За родину», «За хворих»).

Functionality

  • Prayers library (PrayersListRoute) — a flat, scrollable categorized list with a gilt-bordered search pill that case-insensitively filters every group by title + meta; when no row matches, a «Нічого не знайдено» empty state renders. Rows deep-link out to the rosary, the psalter reader at today's kathisma, the full 20-kathisma list, the canon and akathist readers, and the home prayer-session carousel.
  • Rosary — Вервиця (RosaryRoute) — a single Koin entry hosting two stages that swap composables internally: RosaryOverviewScreen (ring + decade pins, center stat block, gilt resume card) and RosaryFocusScreen, the active prayer session. The focus stage shows a live per-second timer, an Apple-Watch-style ActivityRing (arc fills Pater → 10 Aves → Gloria, fraction = (1 + aveCount + glorias) / 12), tap-to-advance phase progression, and a decade-complete bloom overlay. RosaryViewModel keeps timers and Room-persisted decades consistent across both stages, seeds today's mystery group by day-of-week, and ties completion into StreakRepository.
  • Akathist reader (AkafistRoute) — a full-screen reader for the Акафіст до Пресвятої Богородиці (the Великий Акафіст), rendering numbered Кондак / Ікос sections in Cormorant body type.
    The bundled akathist text is clergy-review-pending: when AkathistContent.clergyReviewPending is true the hero shows an italic faded-ochre notice — «Текст очікує на звірення духовенством.» The UA text is transcribed from a cited public-domain UGCC source but awaits clergy verification.
  • Canon of the Day (CanonOfDayRoute) — a reader keyed on the current ISO weekday; CanonOfDayViewModel picks the day's canon at construction. Items are typed (irmos / tropar / katavasia rendered as labelled Cormorant blocks; rubric as italic faded-ochre). Sunday ships as a single rubric-only placeholder (the Octoechos tone-canon).
  • Generic section reader (PrayerSectionReaderRoute) — a long-scroll reader over any molutovnyk_parsed.json section (e.g. section_ранішні_молитви), driven by PrayerSectionReaderViewModel from a URL-encoded sectionId nav arg. Renders per-item titles plus rubric / prayer / text bodies, with Loading / Ready / Empty / Error states.

Navigation flow

The Молитви tab (MolytovnykRoutes.Prayers = "prayers", registered in MolytovnykBottomNav with the VolunteerActivism icon) lands on PrayersListRoute. The list stays on the bottom nav so users can hop between tabs. From its rows, navigation fans out to the rosary and to the modal readers:

  • Вервиця → rosary (MolytovnykRoutes.Rosary, @Modal). One route hosts both rosary stages; the overview keeps a back affordance, and the focus stage runs full-bleed with the bottom nav suppressed. onClose is wired to popBackStack().
  • Канон дня → canon/day (@Modal); Акафіст Богородиці → akathist/theotokos (@Modal); daily/non-daily prayer rows → prayer/section/{sectionId} (@Modal, URL-encoded id) or the home carousel at prayer/{routineId} (morning / evening / daily).
  • Link out to Psalter — the «Псалтир» row deep-links into the kathisma reader at today's kathisma (psalter/reader/{n}), and «Усі 20 кафизм» navigates to psalter/all (the existing KathismaListRoute in :feature:psalter-v2).

Modal status comes from the KSP-generated MODAL_ROUTES set (each constant carries @Modal); MainActivityV2 reads it to hide the bottom nav. Back from any modal reader pops to the Prayers list; back from the rosary focus stage exits the session (saving progress) and returns to the overview.
